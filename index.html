The Anticipation Game — a systems-thinking game about acting under uncertainty.

You govern a town facing an approaching crisis. Early on you can act freely but you barely know what's coming. As the crisis nears, the forecast sharpens — but your power to act drains away. Act too early and you may waste resources on a false alarm; act too late and it's already here.
